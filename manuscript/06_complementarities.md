# Complementarity: what each programme supplies {#sec:complementarities}

The four complementarities are not identifications and not failures. Each is a
case where one programme supplies a structure that another needs and does not
have, without either reducing to the other. They are the constructive part of the
paper.

## Synergetics supplies the accounting {#sec:quadray}

The carrier of [@def:carrier] is a graph. To draw it, to count its shells, and to
state the volume of the polyhedra it bounds, a graph is not enough: a metric is
needed, and the choice of metric decides which numbers come out exact. Synergetics
supplies one in which they all do.

::: {#prop:quadray-ivm .proposition}
The twelve quadray neighbour moves of [@eq:neighbours] are distinct, all have
squared norm {{IVM_NEIGHBOR_SQUARED_DISTANCE}}, and their minimum pairwise squared
distance is also {{IVM_NEIGHBOR_SQUARED_DISTANCE}}, so the twelve form a kissing
configuration of radius $\sqrt{2}$. The shells have
`{{IVM_SHELL_COUNTS}}` sites for $k = 0,\dots,4$ and cumulative counts
`{{IVM_CUMULATIVE_COUNTS}}`. The tetravolume ratios include
`{{IVM_VOLUME_RATIOS}}`.
:::

The complementarity is exact and one-directional. OPH's dynamics does not need
quadray coordinates, and the FEP does not need a lattice. But the moment the
carrier is drawn, or its shells are counted as geometry rather than as graph
distance, the quadray convention is what makes the statements exact rather than
approximate. [@fig:ivm] shows the shells and the volume ratios.

cumulative count overlaid; the first shell is the twelve-around-one

The kissing number is the point at which the two programmes touch. The carrier has
{{CARRIER_PORTS}} ports of degree $5$; the IVM has {{IVM_NEIGHBOR_MOVES}}
neighbours at squared distance {{IVM_NEIGHBOR_SQUARED_DISTANCE}}; and the
geometric icosahedron's adjacency is isomorphic to the derived carrier adjacency,
which the probe checks by constructing a witness. The coincidence is not a
numerological one: the icosahedron's twelve vertices are a kissing configuration,
and its five-fold coordination is the two-dimensional shadow of the
three-dimensional contact count [@schutte1953dreizehn; @conway1999sphere].

## The same quadratic sequence appears three times {#sec:subdivision}

Fuller's geodesic subdivision and Goldberg's dual construction produce the same
pair of quadratic sequences, and the IVM shell count is one of them.

::: {#prop:subdivision .proposition}
For every frequency $f \ge 1$: the geodesic subdivision of the icosahedron has
$10f^{2}+2$ vertices, $30f^{2}$ edges and $20f^{2}$ faces; its Goldberg dual has
$20f^{2}$ vertices, $30f^{2}$ edges and $10f^{2}+2$ faces. Hence the vertex count
of one is the face count of the other, and both are the IVM shell count of
[@eq:shell].
:::

![Three appearances of one sequence. The geodesic vertex count `10f^2 + 2`, the
Goldberg face count, and the IVM shell count are the same function of the
frequency, while the geodesic face count and the Goldberg vertex count are
`20f^2`. The plot shows both sequences against $f$ and annotates the duality.
Generated by `scripts/make_figures.py` from `geometry/subdivision.py`.](../output/figures/fig_subdivision.png){#fig:subdivision width=85%}

This is the clearest complementarity in the paper, and the least mysterious. The
Euler characteristic of the sphere forces the two counts to swap under duality,
and the IVM shell count is the same quadratic because both count a hexagonal
lattice wrapped around a point. What Synergetics adds is the insistence that the
*volume* accounting is exact in the same convention, which is what makes the
subdivision usable as a discretisation rather than only as a drawing.

## FEP supplies an energy-based learning identity {#sec:equilibrium-propagation}

Equilibrium propagation is the cleanest case in which a free-energy functional is
not merely a bound but a computational device: perturb the output, let the system
relax, and read the gradient from the difference in the relaxed energy
[@scellier2017equilibrium].

::: {#prop:ep .proposition}
At perturbation $\beta$, the closed-form contrast is $-1.24999875$ and the relaxed
contrast is $-1.24999874998$, against an exact gradient of $-1.25$. The
closed-form error is $2.1\times 10^{-11}$ and the distance to the gradient is
$1.2499987498\times 10^{-6}$, matching the predicted $O(\beta)$ scaling.
:::

The complementarity is that the FEP's functional provides the *objective* and the
relaxation dynamics provides the *implementation*, and the identity between them
is exact to the stated order in the perturbation. Neither OPH's repair rule nor
Synergetics' accounting gives such an identity, and neither needs one.

## FEP supplies a Lyapunov function for the replicator {#sec:replicator}

::: {#prop:replicator .proposition}
On the replicator field, the velocity equals a mobility matrix times the gradient
of the potential, $M\nabla F$, with the direct potential rate $0.1344$ agreeing
with the rate computed through the mobility. The field norm is $0.21196$.
:::

The replicator equation is a gradient flow in a non-Euclidean metric
[@taylor1978evolutionary; @hofbauer1998evolutionary], and the free energy is a
Lyapunov function for it. The probe checks the identity, not merely the sign: the
field equals the mobility times the gradient, component by component, and the two
ways of computing the rate of change of the potential agree.

The relevance to the three programmes is that this is the one place where a
"free energy" is unambiguously a Lyapunov function of a dynamics that appears in
population biology rather than in neuroscience. It supplies a template: a
synthesis that wants a free-energy descent for OPH's repairs should look for the
metric in which the repair field is a gradient, and the replicator case shows what
such a metric looks like when it exists.

## What the complements add up to

The four complements partition the labour cleanly. Synergetics supplies a metric
and a volume convention in which the carrier's geometry is exact. The FEP
supplies a functional, a bound, and the two identities — equilibrium propagation
and the metric gradient — that make the functional computationally and
dynamically meaningful. OPH supplies a finite substrate and an explicit local
update that neither of the others has.

The synthesis that the complements suggest is therefore not a reduction but an
assignment: the carrier is OPH's, the functional is the FEP's, and the accounting
is Synergetics'. [@sec:scope] states the limits of that assignment.
