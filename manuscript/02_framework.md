# The three programs and the one object they share, stated in their own vocabularies {#sec:framework}

## The carrier

All three programs, read charitably, make claims about one finite object: a
twelve-vertex, thirty-edge, twenty-face complex. This section fixes that object
and then states each program's mathematics on it.

::: {#def:carrier .definition}
A **patch complex** is a finite simplicial complex $K=(V,E,F)$ with twelve
vertices (*ports*), thirty edges (*seams*) and twenty triangular faces. A **load
configuration** is a map $x\colon V\to\mathbb{Z}$; the *total load* is
$\sum_{v} x_v$. A load configuration is **binary** when $x_v\in\{0,1\}$ for every
port. The **carrier** is the specific complex whose twenty oriented faces are
those of the regular icosahedron under the port chart of [@oph].
:::

The port chart is not decoration. OPH ships the twenty oriented faces, and the
adjacency is a consequence of them: two ports are joined exactly when some face
contains both. This package derives the adjacency that way and then asserts that
the twenty triangles of the derived graph are exactly the twenty faces it started
from, so a transcription error in either direction would be caught.

::: {#prop:carrier .proposition}
The graph obtained from OPH's twenty oriented faces is
{{CARRIER_PORTS}}-regular on {{CARRIER_PORTS}} ports with {{CARRIER_SEAMS}} seams,
and its {{CARRIER_FACES}} triangles are exactly the twenty generating faces.
:::

The adjacency that results is listed in [@tbl:adjacency]; it is reproduced here
because the labelling differs from the common icosahedral labelling in which
port $0$ meets ports $1,2,3,4,6$, and a reader who assumes the common labelling
will not reproduce any downstream number.

of this graph are exactly OPH's twenty oriented faces. {#tbl:adjacency}

The carrier's Laplacian is the standard graph Laplacian
$L = 5I - A$, which is how OPH writes it, and its spectrum is closed-form. The
algebraic connectivity is $5-\sqrt{5}$, and the gap normalised by the total
degree $2\lvert E\rvert$ is $(5-\sqrt{5})/60$.

::: {#prop:spectrum .proposition}
The carrier Laplacian has spectrum
$\{0,\ (5-\sqrt{5})^{\times 3},\ 6^{\times 5},\ (5+\sqrt{5})^{\times 3}\}$, so
$\lambda_1 = 5-\sqrt{5} \approx {{CARRIER_LAMBDA1}}$ and
$\lambda_1/(2\lvert E\rvert) = (5-\sqrt{5})/60 = {{CARRIER_SHELL_GAP}}$.
:::

The multiplicity pattern is the one forced by the icosahedral symmetry: the
trivial representation once, the three-dimensional representation three times,
and so on. [@fig:carrier] draws the carrier and its spectrum.

## OPH: a repair law on the seams

OPH's native dynamics is a local rule that moves integer load across a seam. On a
seam joining ports $a$ and $b$, with $d = x_a - x_b$ and a coin
$c\in\{0,1\}$, the amount moved is

$$
\delta \;=\; \frac{d - (2c-1)\,(\lvert d\rvert \bmod 2)}{2},
\qquad x_a \leftarrow x_a - \delta,\quad x_b \leftarrow x_b + \delta .
$$ {#eq:repair}

The rule is written this way, rather than as "move half the difference", because
it must stay in the integers: the parity correction is what keeps $\delta$
integral when $d$ is odd. The quadratic energy

$$
E(x) \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\sum_{v\in V} x_v^{2}
$$ {#eq:energy}

then falls by a closed-form amount. Conservation and descent are the two
properties everything downstream depends on.

::: {#prop:descent .proposition}
Under [@eq:repair] the total load is conserved and
$\Delta E = -\bigl(d^{2} - (\lvert d\rvert \bmod 2)\bigr)/4$.
:::

At $\lvert d\rvert = 1$ the decrease is exactly zero: the parity correction absorbs the whole
of the move. That single fact is the origin of every obstruction in
[@sec:disagreements], and it is worth stating before the disagreements appear,
because it is a property of the rule rather than of any interpretation of it.

::: {#def:shell .definition}
The **load shell** of total $q$ is the set
$\Sigma_q = \{x\in\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}^{V} : \sum_v x_v = q\}$. The shell of total
two has $\lvert\Sigma_2\rvert = \binom{13}{11} = {{SHELL_TOTAL_TWO}}$ configurations, of
which {{SHELL_BINARY}} are binary.
:::

## FEP: a bound on surprisal

The Free Energy Principle starts from a system with sensory states $o$ and
internal states $s$, and a recognition density $q(s)$. The variational free
energy is

$$
\mathcal{F}[q] \;=\; \mathbb{E}_{q}\bigl[-\log p(o,s)\bigr]
\;-\; \mathbb{E}_{q}\bigl[-\log q(s)\bigr]
\;=\; D_{\mathrm{KL}}\bigl[q(s)\,\|\,p(s\mid o)\bigr] \;-\; \log p(o),
$$ {#eq:vfe}

so that minimising $\mathcal{F}$ over $q$ at fixed $o$ is minimising a divergence,
and $-\mathcal{F}$ is a lower bound on the log evidence, the evidence lower bound
[@friston2010; @friston2007synthese]. The bound is tight exactly when the
recognition density is the posterior; the residual is the divergence.

The linear-Gaussian corner is the one this paper can make exact. Take a jointly
Gaussian model whose precision matrix is a graph Laplacian on $K$, partition the
ports into recorded ports $o$ and free ports $x$, and eliminate $x$. The
variational free energy at the optimum is then a quadratic form in the records
plus a constant:

$$
\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{G}}(o) \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\,o^{\top} S\, o \;+\; \rho(o)
\;+\; \tfrac{1}{2}\log\det L_{xx} \;-\; \tfrac{d_x}{2}\log 2\pi ,
$$ {#eq:gaussian}

where $L_{xx}$ is the free-free block of the Laplacian, $S$ is the Schur
complement of $L_{xx}$ in $L$, and $\rho$ collects the linear terms. The
absolute level of [@eq:gaussian] is gauge-dependent, because a Laplacian has a
kernel; differences of $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{G}}$ between two record
configurations are exact, and that is how the probe uses it.

A **Markov blanket** is a partition of the state into interior $i$, blanket $b$
and exterior $e$ such that the interior and exterior are conditionally
independent given the blanket:

$$
p(i,e\mid b) \;=\; p(i\mid b)\,p(e\mid b).
$$ {#eq:blanket}

[@eq:blanket] is a statement about a probability distribution. It becomes a
statement about dynamics only under further conditions, which is the content of
[@prop:stationary].

## Synergetics: tetravolume accounting

Synergetics replaces Cartesian coordinates with four signed counts whose sum is
free, the **quadray** coordinates. A quadray $q=(a,b,c,d)$ is an equivalence
class,

$$
(a,b,c,d) \;\sim\; (a+t,\,b+t,\,c+t,\,d+t) \qquad \text{for every } t\in\mathbb{R},
$$ {#eq:quadray}

so that a point is determined by differences. In the integer lattice the twelve
nearest neighbours of the origin are the distinct permutations of $(2,1,1,0)$:

$$
\mathcal{N} \;=\; \bigl\{\,\pi(2,1,1,0) \;:\; \pi \text{ a permutation}\,\bigr\},
\qquad \lvert\mathcal{N}\rvert = {{IVM_NEIGHBOR_MOVES}} .
$$ {#eq:neighbours}

This is the twelve-around-one configuration, the *vector equilibrium* of
Synergetics and the kissing configuration of the A3 root lattice
[@conway1999sphere; @schutte1953dreizehn]. Two features make it the natural
carrier for the other two programs. First, the neighbours are at squared distance
{{IVM_NEIGHBOR_SQUARED_DISTANCE}}, so the shell of radius $k$ contains

$$
\lvert\{q : \lVert q\rVert^{2} = k^{2}\cdot 8\}\rvert \;=\; 10k^{2}+2
$$ {#eq:shell}

sites, which is exact and quadratic in $k$ [@quadmath]. Second, the tetravolume of
a lattice simplex is $\lvert\det\rvert/4$, so the unit tetrahedron has volume one
and the standard polyhedra have integer volumes: the cube $3$, the octahedron
$4$, the rhombic dodecahedron $6$, the cuboctahedron $20$ and the truncated
octahedron $96$. No square roots appear anywhere in the accounting, which is the
point of the convention [@fuller1975synergetics; @fuller1979synergetics2;
@edmondson1987fuller].

::: {#prop:ivm .proposition}
The quadray lattice generated by [@eq:neighbours] has {{IVM_NEIGHBOR_MOVES}}
neighbour moves, all at squared distance {{IVM_NEIGHBOR_SQUARED_DISTANCE}}, shells
of `10k^2 + 2` sites with cumulative counts
`{{IVM_CUMULATIVE_COUNTS}}` for $k=0,\dots,4$, and tetravolume ratios including
`{{IVM_VOLUME_RATIOS}}`.
:::

The dictionary is deliberately conservative. It does not say that a port *is* a
quadray site, only that the two play the same role in the three formalisms and
that the resulting numbers must therefore agree where both are defined. Where the
numbers do not agree, [@sec:disagreements] says so. The carrier's counting
invariants — {{CARRIER_TREE_NUMBER}} spanning trees,
{{SHEAF_H1}} sheaf obstructions, {{SYMMETRY_GROUP_ORDER}} automorphisms, and a
census of {{CARRIER_TRIANGLES}} triangles, {{CARRIER_PENTAGONS}} five-cycles,
{{CARRIER_PERFECT_MATCHINGS}} matchings and {{CARRIER_HAMILTONIAN_CYCLES}}
Hamiltonian cycles — are derived from the same complex in
[@sec:correspondences], and each of them is a statement that two vocabularies
inherit rather than assert.
