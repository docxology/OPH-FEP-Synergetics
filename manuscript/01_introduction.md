# Introduction {#sec:introduction}

## Three programs, one question

Observer Patch Holography (OPH) is a discrete reconstruction programme: a finite
complex of patches carries integer load, seams between patches carry a repair
law, and geometry is what the repair statistics look like from inside
[@oph; @dossier2026]. The Free Energy Principle (FEP) is a variational programme:
a system that persists must keep its sensory surprisal bounded, and the bound is
a functional of a recognition density [@friston2010; @friston2013life;
@parr2022active]. Synergetics is a geometric programme: spatial accounting is done
in tetravolumes on a closest-packed lattice generated by twelve equal spheres
around a central one, and the resulting coordinate system needs no irrational
constants [@fuller1975synergetics; @quadmath].

Each program answers a question the others only gesture at. OPH supplies a
finite, exactly specified substrate and an explicit local update. FEP supplies
the functional that a persisting system must be minimising, together with a
statistical notion of a boundary. Synergetics supplies a coordinate system in
which the relevant finite configurations are counted without square roots.

The question this paper asks is narrow enough to be answerable: *where the three
programs make claims about the same finite object, do those claims agree?*
Agreement here means more than analogy. Two claims agree when they can be stated
as functions of the same object and the values coincide.

## What a correspondence is

A correspondence is a record with three parts: a claim, a probe, and a status.
The claim names what OPH, FEP and Synergetics each say. The probe is executable
code that computes the values the claim rests on. The status is one of four
terms, used in a fixed sense throughout [@sec:method].

`exact` means the probe reproduces the claimed value exactly, in integers or
rationals, or to floating-point equality with the closed form. `exact-on-domain`
means the identification holds on a stated domain, typically the linear-Gaussian
corner, and the probe asserts both the identity on that domain and its failure
off it. `obstructed` means the identification fails, and the probe exhibits the
failure with the same precision as a success. `not-reproduced` means the source
asserts a quantity that its text does not determine, and the probe records the
gap rather than inventing a value.

This vocabulary is the paper's main methodological commitment. An obstructed
correspondence is a result, not a defect. It says where the three programmes
cannot be welded together, and therefore where a synthesis has to choose.

## Contributions

The contributions are the following.

1. A derived carrier. OPH's twelve-port adjacency is recovered from its twenty
   oriented faces rather than transcribed from a table, and the derivation is
   checked against an independent OPH consequence [@sec:framework].
2. A closed-form spectrum and gap for that carrier, with the gap expressed as a
   ratio of exact surds [@prop:spectrum].
3. A complete census of the native repair law over the {{SHELL_TOTAL_TWO}}
   configurations of total load two: {{SHELL_STRICT_DESCENT}} strict descents,
   {{SHELL_ZERO_DESCENT}} zero descents, {{SHELL_NOOP}} no-op moves
   [@prop:census]. The zero-descent moves are OPH's obstructed premise PR-01 made
   executable.
4. A variational equivalence on the linear-Gaussian domain, stated as an identity
   between a harmonic-extension residual and a Schur complement [@prop:ve].
5. Exact thresholds for three dynamical questions: the D-stability onset
   `{{D_STABILITY_ROUTH_ONSET}}` [@prop:dstability], the mediator threshold
   `{{MEDIATOR_THRESHOLD}}` [@prop:mediator], and the solenoidal family's free
   entropy production [@prop:solenoid].
6. An exact correspondence with Synergetics' IVM: twelve neighbour moves at
   squared distance {{IVM_NEIGHBOR_SQUARED_DISTANCE}}, shells of
   `10k^2 + 2` sites, and the tetravolume ratios of the standard polyhedra
   [@prop:ivm].
7. A provenance discipline in which every source claim is pinned to a commit and
   every reimplementation cites the identifier it reimplements [@sec:reproducibility].

Items 1 to 6 are executable: each is a function, and each section below reports
the values that function returns.

## What this paper does not do

It does not claim that OPH is a free energy principle, or that FEP is a
holographic theory, or that Synergetics predicts either. It tests identifications
that have been proposed [@dossier2026; @proposal2026] and reports which survive.
It does not reproduce two quantities that the source literature states without
specifying the protocol that would determine them [@sec:scope]. It does not
compare any of the three against laboratory data; OPH's own empirical status
document is explicit that no retained comparison selects its record law, and this
paper inherits that situation rather than resolving it [@oph].

## Related work and positioning

The FEP literature contains its own internal disagreements, and this paper uses
them as evidence rather than as noise. The Markov blanket is a statistical
conditional-independence structure [@pearl1988probabilistic], not automatically a
dynamical object [@bruineberg2022emperor], and technical objections to parts of
the free energy principle have been stated precisely [@biehl2021technical]. The
variational free energy is a bound, and its exactness conditions matter
[@friston2007synthese; @sajid2021demystified]. Bayesian mechanics gives the
stationary-process reading of the same mathematics
[@dacosta2021bayesian; @parr2020blankets], and the discrete-state synthesis
supplies the notation for the active-inference corner
[@dacosta2021discrete].

Synergetics has a small technical literature that states its conventions
carefully [@edmondson1987fuller], and the geodesic subdivision it motivates is the
Goldberg construction [@goldberg1937multisymmetric] on the regular icosahedron
[@coxeter1973regular]. The lattice that results is the root lattice A3 in the
closest-packing literature [@conway1999sphere], whose kissing number twelve was
established by Schütte and van der Waerden [@schutte1953dreizehn].

The tools this paper uses have long pedigrees, and it claims none of them: the
algebraic connectivity of a graph [@fiedler1973connectivity], the Routh–Hurwitz
criterion for a cubic [@routh1877stability; @hurwitz1895bedingungen], Lyapunov
stability [@khalil2002nonlinear], contraction analysis [@lohmiller1998contraction],
energy landscapes in neural networks [@hopfield1982neural], the information
measures the surprises are built from [@shannon1948communication;
@kullback1951information; @amari2016information], topological signal processing
[@ghrist2014elementary], the Markov-blanket literature [@kirchhoff2018blankets],
and the predictive-processing reading of the free energy principle
[@clark2013whatever; @friston2017process; @friston2019particular].

The contribution here is not a new theory but a decision procedure: a
correspondence either executes or it does not, and the paper reports which.
