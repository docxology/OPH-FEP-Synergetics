# Abstract {#sec:abstract}

We test correspondences among Observer Patch Holography (OPH), the Free Energy
Principle (FEP), and Fuller's Synergetics. The comparison uses OPH's finite
integer-load observer [@oph; @mueller2026observers], variational inference
[@friston2010; @parr2022active], and Synergetics' lattice and volume conventions
[@fuller1975synergetics; @edmondson1987fuller]. An executable registry records
{{REGISTRY_ENTRIES}} scoped claims: {{REGISTRY_ISOMORPHISMS}} isomorphisms,
{{REGISTRY_DISAGREEMENTS}} disagreements, and
{{REGISTRY_COMPLEMENTARITIES}} complementarities. Each probe reports the
values and comparisons that support its verdict.

The carrier graph is recovered from OPH's oriented faces. Its Laplacian
governs the mean repair and supplies the precision structure of a Gaussian
model. With a proper record prior, the harmonic extension and posterior mean
coincide, and minimised variational free energy equals record surprisal
[@mueller2026agreement]. On the binary load shell with a uniform reference,
the law of one native repair is the reference conditioned on the information
the repair preserves. The resulting random-seam Dirichlet form is the mean
conditional variance over those seam records. The total-load-two census also
separates two rewrite relations. Ungated repair is confluent and can cycle.
Restricting repair to broken seams gives termination with multiple reachable
normal forms.

The geometry yields exact counts, effective resistances, and volumes.
On the carrier, squared Euclidean chords recover the contribution of the
lowest positive Laplacian modes to Gaussian contrast variance up to scale.
The full variance has a higher-dimensional embedding.
The extended probes distinguish first entry from binary equilibrium, give an
exact graph-distance reduction, and count rooted forests through conditional
Gaussian precision. A greedy record choice misses every optimal triple.
Planar dual edge covariances complement one another, and the FCC shell splits
one graph-distance class into two different contrast variances.
Across both graphs, contrast variance gives an exact Markov record of
binary repair, while the observed centroid retains memory despite matching
all lag covariances of a Gaussian process. The weighted dual identity
requires reciprocal conductances up to a global scale. The geometries also give different
quadratic coefficient variances and different Gaussian screening by
central planes.
Beyond the two shells, distance regularity is equivalent to an exact
distance quotient for fair binary repair on regular graphs. Equal variance
fails as a Markov record on a periodic FCC graph. Reciprocal duality leaves a
harmonic projector on a torus, and a sum constraint can couple half-fields
across a grounded geometric separator.
Fuller's Jitterbug connects the cuboctahedral and icosahedral joint
configurations with fixed-length bars. The icosahedral hull adds a
matching of short diagonals. In the weighted seam model, a unique
conductance on that matching preserves Gaussian lag covariance as
the coordinates move between the landmarks.
Cuboctahedral and icosahedral configurations attain the same kissing number
with different contacts among the outer spheres. The cuboctahedral gauge
determines lattice coordination shells, and equal-covariance Gaussian
divergence measures squared Euclidean displacement. The two functions split
the lattice into different level sets. These results concern specified finite
models and their declared regimes. The registry, source pins, and manuscript
bindings provide the evidence needed to rerun the comparisons.
