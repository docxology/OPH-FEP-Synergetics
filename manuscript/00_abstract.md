# Abstract: three programmes, one carrier, and a registry that executes every claim {#sec:abstract}

Three research programs describe observers in incompatible vocabularies. Observer
Patch Holography builds spacetime from a finite complex of patches joined by seams,
with a local repair law that moves load across those seams [@oph]. The Free Energy
Principle derives the behaviour of a self-maintaining system from a variational
bound on surprisal [@friston2010; @parr2022active]. Fuller's Synergetics
axiomatises a closest-packed, tetravolume-counted geometry in which the
twelve-around-one configuration is the unit of spatial accounting
[@fuller1975synergetics; @edmondson1987fuller].

This paper reports what happens when the three are made executable against one
another. A registry of {{REGISTRY_ENTRIES}} correspondences was built, each one a
claim scoped to a pair of programmes and carrying a probe that either reproduces
an exact value or records the failure precisely: {{REGISTRY_ISOMORPHISMS}}
isomorphisms, {{REGISTRY_DISAGREEMENTS}} disagreements and
{{REGISTRY_COMPLEMENTARITIES}} complementarities. By scope,
{{REGISTRY_OPH_FEP}} claims relate OPH to the FEP,
{{REGISTRY_SYNERGETICS_OPH}} relate Synergetics to OPH,
{{REGISTRY_FEP_SYNERGETICS}} relate the FEP to Synergetics, and
{{REGISTRY_THREE_WAY}} entries need all three vocabularies at once. The load-bearing
agreements are structural. OPH's carrier is a {{CARRIER_PORTS}}-port,
{{CARRIER_SEAMS}}-seam, {{CARRIER_FACES}}-face complex whose adjacency is
recovered from its own oriented faces, and its Laplacian spectrum is closed-form,
with spectral gap `{{CARRIER_LAMBDA1}}`. The native repair law conserves load
and lowers a quadratic energy by a closed-form amount, and over the
{{SHELL_TOTAL_TWO}}-configuration load shell it produces {{SHELL_STRICT_DESCENT}}
strict descents, {{SHELL_ZERO_DESCENT}} zero descents and {{SHELL_NOOP}} no-op
moves out of {{SHELL_TRANSITIONS}} transitions.

Counting the same object turns out to add identifications that no dynamical
reading supplies. The seam graph has {{CARRIER_TREE_NUMBER}} spanning trees, an
integer that survives the surds in its own spectrum; the scalar sheaf over the
complex has {{SHEAF_H0}} global section up to a constant and {{SHEAF_H1}}
obstructions, which are the cycle rank of the carrier; the automorphism group has
order {{SYMMETRY_GROUP_ORDER}}, splitting into {{SYMMETRY_ROTATIONS}} rotations
and {{SYMMETRY_REFLECTIONS}} reflections, transitive on ports, seams and faces,
with the descent census invariant across all {{SYMMETRY_CONFIGURATIONS}}
configurations and {{SYMMETRY_ORBITS}} orbits under it. Its subgraphs are counted
the same way: {{CARRIER_TRIANGLES}} triangles, exactly the faces the port chart
was derived from, {{CARRIER_PENTAGONS}} five-cycles for the dual solid's
vertices, {{CARRIER_PERFECT_MATCHINGS}} perfect matchings, and
{{CARRIER_HAMILTONIAN_CYCLES}} Hamiltonian cycles, with a curvature of
{{CARRIER_TOTAL_DEFECT}} pi that is Gauss-Bonnet exactly and a resistance ladder
whose exact fractions sum to a Kirchhoff index of
{{RESISTANCE_KIRCHHOFF_INDEX}}. The FEP's Gaussian
reading of the same matrix puts conditional independence exactly on the seams,
with a Markov blanket of {{GMRF_BLANKET_SIZE}} neighbours, and its divergence
makes one lattice step cost {{KL_ONE_STEP}} nats, so the information cost of a
lattice site is half its squared distance in Synergetics' own metric.
Synergetics' accounting then extends to the carrier's solid, which measures
{{ICOSAHEDRON_TETRAVOLUME}} tetravolumes against the integral table
{{TETRAVOLUME_INTEGRAL_SOLIDS}}, and to the twelve-around-one cell, whose
{{VORONOI_VERTICES}} vertices, {{VORONOI_FACES}} faces and
{{VORONOI_EDGES}} edges give tetravolume {{PACKING_CELL_TETRAVOLUME}} and the
packing density {{PACKING_DENSITY}}.

The load-bearing disagreements are equally structural. The zero-descent moves
are the executable content of OPH's own obstructed premise PR-01: the native law
is not confluent, so an accepted repair sequence is not a path to a unique normal
form. A pairwise reduction of a boundary exists only above a mediator threshold
`{{MEDIATOR_THRESHOLD}}`; a stationary blanket is a kinematic identity rather than
a dynamical attractor; the solenoidal family leaves a stationary blanket's
entropy production free; and four of the obstructions are failures of counting
rather than of dynamics: twelve vertices do not determine a solid, a shell index
is not a function of the free energy, a neighbourhood is not a lattice shell, and
the phrase "twelve around one" does not determine which solid is meant.
Synergetics contributes the one geometry in which the other two can be drawn, not
a derivation of either.

Two quantities asserted in the source literature are reported as not reproduced,
and the scope limits are stated rather than trimmed. Every number in this
manuscript is bound to the function that produces it, every claim is executed by
`oph-fep-synergetics verify` (or narrowed with `--pair` and `--kind`), and the
graphical abstract on the preceding pages states the same results as four drawn
panels.
