# Abstract {#sec:abstract}

Three research programs describe observers in incompatible vocabularies. Observer
Patch Holography builds spacetime from a finite complex of patches joined by seams,
with a local repair law that moves load across those seams [@oph]. The Free Energy
Principle derives the behaviour of a self-maintaining system from a variational
bound on surprisal [@friston2010; @parr2022active]. Fuller's Synergetics
axiomatises a closest-packed, tetravolume-counted geometry in which the
twelve-around-one configuration is the unit of spatial accounting
[@fuller1975synergetics; @edmondson1987fuller].

This paper reports what happens when the three are made executable against one
another. A registry of {{REGISTRY_ENTRIES}} correspondences was built, each one a
claim with a probe that either reproduces an exact value or records the failure
precisely: {{REGISTRY_ISOMORPHISMS}} isomorphisms,
{{REGISTRY_DISAGREEMENTS}} disagreements and {{REGISTRY_COMPLEMENTARITIES}}
complementarities. The load-bearing agreements are structural. OPH's carrier is
a {{CARRIER_PORTS}}-port, {{CARRIER_SEAMS}}-seam, {{CARRIER_FACES}}-face complex
whose adjacency is recovered from its own oriented faces, and its Laplacian
spectrum is closed-form, with spectral gap `{{CARRIER_SHELL_GAP}}`. The native
repair law conserves load and lowers a quadratic energy by a closed-form amount,
and over the {{SHELL_TOTAL_TWO}}-configuration load shell it produces
{{SHELL_STRICT_DESCENT}} strict descents, {{SHELL_ZERO_DESCENT}} zero descents and
{{SHELL_NOOP}} no-op moves out of {{SHELL_TRANSITIONS}} transitions.

The load-bearing disagreements are equally structural. The zero-descent moves
are the executable content of OPH's own obstructed premise PR-01: the native law
is not confluent, so an accepted repair sequence is not a path to a unique normal
form. A pairwise reduction of a boundary exists only above a mediator threshold
`{{MEDIATOR_THRESHOLD}}`; a stationary blanket is a kinematic identity rather than
a dynamical attractor; and the solenoidal family leaves a stationary blanket's
entropy production free. Synergetics contributes the one geometry in which the
other two can be drawn, not a derivation of either.

Two quantities asserted in the source literature are reported as not reproduced,
and the scope limits are stated rather than trimmed. Every number in this
manuscript is bound to the function that produces it.
