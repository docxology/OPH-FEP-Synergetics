## Recovering the port chart from faces {#sec:port-chart}

The derivation in [@prop:carrier] returns {{CARRIER_PORTS}} ports,
{{CARRIER_SEAMS}} seams and {{CARRIER_FACES}} faces, with degree
{{CARRIER_DEGREE}} at each port. The triangles of the derived graph match
the vertex sets of the oriented source faces, and the derived adjacency
matches the declared adjacency. The triangle check detects an extra or missing
clique. The comparison with the declared, labelled adjacency is a separate
check. Face orientation is checked separately in [@sec:symmetry].

## The spectrum is closed-form {#sec:spectrum}

[@prop:spectrum] gives the full spectrum. The probe reports the eigenvalues as
`{{CARRIER_LAMBDA1}}` three times, six five times, and
$5+\sqrt{5} \approx {{SPECTRAL_LAMBDA_MAX}}$ three times, with the zero eigenvalue once, and checks that the Laplacian
really is $5I - A$ rather than a normalised variant.

The gap is the quantity that carries dynamical meaning. Normalising by the total
degree,

$$
\gamma \;=\; \frac{\lambda_1}{2\lvert E\rvert} \;=\; \frac{5-\sqrt{5}}{60} \;=\; {{CARRIER_SHELL_GAP}},
$$ {#eq:gamma}

gives the gap of the **mean repair operator**. For a uniform seam and a fair
coin, the operator on port loads is the symmetric stochastic matrix

$$
P \;=\; I - \frac{L}{2\lvert E\rvert},
$$ {#eq:chain}

whose rows sum to one and whose entries are non-negative. Its second eigenvalue
is $1-\gamma = {{CERTIFICATE_SECOND_EIGENVALUE}}$ and its induced infinity
norm is {{CERTIFICATE_INFINITY_NORM}}. These are the values checked by
`certificate-species`. The operator acts on expected loads at ports. The
binary-shell Markov kernel acts on distributions over configurations, and
[@sec:pr01] checks its gap separately.

The chain is also what the integer rule does on average, and the probe checks
that on the rule itself. Averaging [@eq:repair] over every seam and both coins,
from each of the {{SHELL_TOTAL_TWO}} configurations, returns
$x - Lx/(2\lvert E\rvert)$ in exact rationals: the mean current across a seam is
half its mismatch for either parity. OPH states the same identity as one gradient
step of the seam-disagreement energy under its uniform calibration [@oph], so
[@eq:chain] is derived from the law and the Laplacian is the law's own mean
generator.

## The shell is counted exactly {#sec:shell}

[@def:shell] gives the count. The probe reports
$\lvert\Sigma_2\rvert = \binom{13}{11} = {{SHELL_TOTAL_TWO}}$ total-two configurations and
{{SHELL_BINARY}} binary ones, and checks the enumeration against the closed forms
$\binom{13}{11}$ (stars and bars: two units of load over twelve ports) and
$\binom{12}{2}$ (two occupied ports out of twelve), which share no code with it.
The check is worth having because the enumerated set is the one that the repair
census below partitions.

## The exact integer repair law {#sec:repair-law}

The `native-repair-law` correspondence reimplements OPH's rule on exact integers
and checks it over all {{SHELL_TRANSITIONS}} transitions of the
{{SHELL_TOTAL_TWO}}-state shell. For [@eq:repair], the energy decrease
$E(x)-E(x')$ is $\bigl(d^{2} - (\lvert d\rvert \bmod 2)\bigr)/4$.
The probe compares that formula with the energy computed before and after the
update. OPH records the selection of a native record law by laboratory
comparison as an open empirical question [@oph].

::: {#prop:ve .proposition}
Let $L$ be the carrier Laplacian partitioned into recorded ports $o$ and free
ports $x$, equip $o$ with the prior in [@eq:gaussian], and let
$x^{*}(o) = -L_{xx}^{-1} L_{xo}\, o$ be the harmonic extension of the records.
The posterior has mean $x^{*}(o)$ and precision $L_{xx}$. At that posterior,
the variational free energy takes the value $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm G}(o)=-\ln p(o)$
[@mueller2026agreement, theorem 7], with

$$
S \;=\; L_{oo} - L_{ox} L_{xx}^{-1} L_{xo},
\qquad
\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{G}}(o) - \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{G}}(o_0)
\;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(o^{\top} S\, o - o_0^{\top} S\, o_0\bigr)
\;+\; \rho(o) - \rho(o_0).
$$ {#eq:ve}
:::

The probe uses two antipodal carrier records with prior precision
{{FE_RECORD_PRIOR_PRECISION}} and repeats the checks on a four-node chain.
It computes the settled interior of [@eq:ve] three ways that share no linear solve.
The harmonic extension solves the interior block. The constrained minimiser
solves one saddle-point (KKT) system for $\min \tfrac{1}{2}z^{\top}Lz$ subject to
$z_o = o$ over all coordinates and a multiplier per record. The prior contributes
a fixed term at fixed records, so the same constrained solution minimises the
relative entropy of a mean shift at fixed joint precision. This is the
constrained relative-entropy reading of OPH's premise A3.
And the predictive-coding flow `perceive` reaches the same point in
{{VE_ITERATIONS}} iterations with residual {{VE_RESIDUAL}}, from every start
tried. The probe also evaluates the free energy at the posterior directly, as
energy minus entropy, and finds it equal to the surprisal: the divergence in
[@eq:vfe] has numerical residual {{VE_EXCESS_RESIDUAL}} because the bound is
tight at the posterior.

The boundary control clamps a single record on an oriented seam with an
asymmetric spring. The energy is piecewise quadratic, with twice the stiffness
for positive disagreement. Its unique minimiser is at zero disagreement,
while numerical integration and a half-Gaussian calculation give a negative
posterior mean. The equality of minimiser and posterior mean in [@prop:ve]
depends on the Gaussian assumption.

## Each native repair is exact conditioning {#sec:conditioning}

The identifications above read one object in two vocabularies. This one relates
the native law itself to a free-energy functional, and it is exact
[@mueller2026agreement; @mueller2026observers].

A repair of a seam conserves the load on the seam and touches no other port. The
seam's total load together with every load off the seam is therefore the same
before and after the repair. That pair is the datum the repair leaves visible,
and the configurations that share it are its fibre.

::: {#prop:conditioning .proposition}
Let $u$ be the uniform law on the {{SHELL_BINARY}} binary configurations and
$K_e$ the law of one native repair of the seam $e$ under its fair coin. For every
binary configuration $x$ and every seam $e$, $K_e(x,\cdot)$ is $u$ conditioned on
the datum $o$ of $x$ at $e$. With $\pi(s,o) = u(s)$ for $s$ in the fibre of $o$,
$K_e(x,\cdot)$ is the unique minimiser of

$$
\mathcal{F}[q] \;=\; \mathbb{E}_{q}\bigl[\ln q(s) - \ln \pi(s,o)\bigr]
$$ {#eq:fibre-vfe}

over the laws $q$ on the fibre, and the minimum is the surprisal $-\ln\pi(o)$ of
the datum.
:::

The probe compares the two sides of [@prop:conditioning] in all
{{CONDITIONING_CASES}} cases, both orientations of every seam from every binary
configuration, by routes that share no code: the integer rule on one side, the
reference and the datum on the other. A seam that holds one unit has a fibre of
two configurations and the repair resamples the unit's side with equal weights,
which happens in {{CONDITIONING_ONE_UNIT_CASES}} cases; every other seam has a
fibre of one. The surprisal is `{{CONDITIONING_SURPRISAL_ONE_UNIT}}` in the first
case and `{{CONDITIONING_SURPRISAL_SETTLED}}` in the second. Evaluated on
[@eq:fibre-vfe], a coin with any bias pays exactly its divergence from the fair
coin above that minimum, and a rule that always waits or always swaps pays
`{{CONDITIONING_DETERMINISTIC_EXCESS}}`.

Every law $p$ on the shell obeys
$D(p\,\|\,u) = D(p\,\|\,pK_e) + D(pK_e\,\|\,u)$.
Within each fibre, $pK_e$ has the probability mass of $p$ and the
conditional distribution of $u$. Splitting the logarithm of $p/u$
proves the identity. The probe checks it on a mixed state for every seam.
The conditioning interpretation is recorded as OPH premise `PR-07` [@oph].

The status is `exact-on-domain`, and the probe asserts the boundary. With the
uniform law on all {{SHELL_TOTAL_TWO}} configurations as reference the identity
fails at the doubled configurations, on the {{CONDITIONING_OFF_SHELL_FAILURES}}
seams that meet a doubled port out of {{CONDITIONING_OFF_SHELL_CASES}} off-shell
cases: the repair moves one unit across with certainty, and conditioning would
spread the weight over three configurations.

In the FEP's vocabulary each native repair is a perception step whose recognition
density is the exact posterior, with the uniform reference as prior and the
repaired datum as observation, so the bound of [@eq:vfe] is tight at every step.
The functional each step minimises is [@eq:fibre-vfe]. It is a different object
from the load energy of [@eq:energy], which [@sec:pr01] shows to be constant along
the binary shell while every step there is exact inference. Registered as
`repair-is-conditioning`.

::: {#prop:binary-conditional-variance .proposition}
Let $u$ be uniform on the binary load shell, $Y_e$ the datum preserved by seam
$e$, and $P$ the native attempt operator with uniform seam selection and a fair
coin. For every real observable $f$ on the shell,
$$
\langle f,(I-P)f\rangle_u
= \frac{1}{\lvert E\rvert}\sum_{e\in E}\mathbb E_u[\operatorname{Var}_u(f\mid Y_e)].
$$
:::

By [@prop:conditioning], the single-seam operator is the Gibbs projection
$K_e f=\mathbb E_u[f\mid Y_e]$ [@chlebicka2025solidarity]. Its squared
projection error is the expected conditional variance. Averaging over seams
gives [@prop:binary-conditional-variance]. The executable comparison enumerates
native attempts and independently builds the variance coefficients from
{{REPAIR_VARIANCE_FIBRES}} two-state fibres. For the indicator of one adjacent
occupied pair it gives energy {{REPAIR_VARIANCE_INDICATOR}}. Registered as
`binary-conditional-variance`.

## The free-energy dictionary has a precise scope {#sec:dictionary}

Mueller distinguishes five kinds of surprise {{PAPER_FIVE_SURPRISES}}
[@mueller2026agreement]. The linear-Gaussian probe reports three of them
in [@tbl:surprises]. Its record-change Bayesian surprise compares
two posteriors with the same covariance, each conditioned on a different
record. Bayesian surprise for a single update in the sense of Itti and Baldi
compares the posterior to the prior for that update
[@itti2009bayesian, equation 4]. The registry entry is `free-energy-dictionary`.

| Quantity | Definition | Value in the probe model |
|:---------|:-----------|:-------------------------|
| numerical surprise | residual norm $\lVert g\rVert$ at the old rest | {{FE_NUMERICAL}} |
| Record-change Bayesian surprise | $\tfrac{1}{2} g^{\top} L_{xx}^{-1} g$ | {{FE_BAYESIAN}} |
| record surprisal | $-\ln p(o)$ under the proper joint model | {{FE_SURPRISAL}} |
| variational free energy | [@eq:vfe] at the posterior | {{FE_VARIATIONAL}} |

: Three surprises in the linear-Gaussian carrier probe. The residual norm and
Bayesian surprise describe a change of records. Record surprisal uses the new
records alone. The sandwich bound brackets the Bayesian value
[@mueller2026agreement, theorem 7 and table 7]. {#tbl:surprises}

The probe reports a sandwich with lower bound {{FE_SANDWICH_LOWER}} and upper
bound {{FE_SANDWICH_UPPER}}, and the Bayesian value {{FE_BAYESIAN}} lies inside
it. The energy drop at the new records and the divergence between posteriors
give the same value by independent routes. The variational free energy equals
record surprisal at the posterior and exceeds it by the divergence from the
posterior away from that optimum.

The asymmetric seam gives a check outside this domain. Ground one port and
read the other with a symmetric record prior. Records of opposite sign and
equal magnitude have different surprisals, because the prior and the
normaliser cancel in their difference and the seam energies do not agree.
The centred quadratic record formula cannot represent this difference.

## A sheaf computes the same Laplacian {#sec:sheaf}

The scalar carrier construction instantiates the cellular sheaf formulation of
agreement [@mueller2026agreement, sec. 7.2 and theorem 7].

::: {#prop:sheaf .proposition}
Give every port the stalk $\mathbb{R}$ and every seam the identity restriction.
The resulting cellular sheaf on $K$ has {{CARRIER_SEAMS}} edges,
Laplacian equal to the carrier Laplacian, one-dimensional space of global
sections (the constants), and injective record maps at every port.
:::

The probe checks injectivity for each single port. The chain control also has
constant global sections. Recording a chain node separates these sections.
The empty record map sends all of them to the same value. Registered as
`sheaf-agreement`.

The sheaf formulation is the one that generalises. A scalar identity sheaf is
the simplest case; the same construction with non-identity restrictions is how
the FEP's precision structure would enter, and the harmonic-extension identity of
[@prop:ve] is the same computation written in sheaf language
[@hansen2019sheaves; @robinson2014topological]. The agreement reported here is
therefore not a coincidence of two implementations but a statement that the
carrier's Laplacian is the coboundary Laplacian of a sheaf, which is the bridge
[@proposal2026] proposed.

## The carrier counts its own spanning trees {#sec:tree-number}

::: {#prop:tree .proposition}
Delete any row and the matching column of the carrier Laplacian. The determinant
of the remaining $11 \times 11$ matrix is an integer, and by Kirchhoff's
matrix-tree theorem [@kirchhoff1847auflosung] that integer is the number of
spanning trees of the seam graph:

$$
\tau(K) \;=\; {{CARRIER_TREE_NUMBER}} \;=\; \frac{1}{12}\prod_{i=2}^{12}\lambda_i .
$$ {#eq:tree}
:::

The probe computes the grounded determinant over the rationals. Forming the
product in [@eq:tree] with the closed spectrum in [@prop:spectrum] gives the
same integer because the factors $5\pm\sqrt{5}$ pair to give rational products.
The product from a numerical eigensolve provides a further check.

The grounded minor $L_g$ is the precision on the remaining coordinates when
one port is fixed to zero. The Gaussian integral of
$\exp(-x^\top L_g x/2)$ is $(2\pi)^{\dim(L_g)/2}/\sqrt{\tau(K)}$.
The probe checks the same log integral by Cholesky factorisation.

## The seam graph gives the proper Gaussian's conditional dependencies {#sec:gmrf}

Use $L+I/n$ as the precision of a proper Gaussian field on the $n$ carrier
ports. The positive diagonal shift grounds the constant mode and leaves the
off-diagonal entries unchanged. The precision's zeros determine conditional
independence [@dempster1972covariance; @rue2005gaussian]:
two ports are independent given the rest exactly when their precision entry
vanishes. The nonzero off-diagonal entries therefore give the seam adjacency,
with {{GMRF_ORDERED_PAIRS}} ordered seam entries in the
${{CARRIER_PORTS}} \times {{CARRIER_PORTS}}$ matrix. The probe separately
conditions every pair using the full proper covariance and recovers the
same dependence graph.

Every seam's pairwise precision is {{GMRF_PAIRWISE_PRECISION}}. A port's Markov
blanket consists of its {{GMRF_BLANKET_SIZE}} neighbours. For the separate
intrinsic field centred to sum to zero, the covariance is $L^+$ and each port's
variance is {{GMRF_MARGINAL_VARIANCE}}, exactly {{GMRF_MARGINAL_VARIANCE_EXACT}}.
Vertex transitivity gives this value by dividing the reciprocal spectrum sum
by $n$. The probe computes the sum over the rationals as
$\operatorname{tr}\bigl((L + J/n)^{-1}\bigr) - 1$, where $J$ is the all-ones
matrix.

Conditioning the proper Gaussian on all other ports gives the graph result.
For the centred intrinsic field the same conditioning fixes the sum of the
remaining pair, including for nonseams. [@sec:stationary] separately tests
whether a blanket in a stationary density constrains the dynamics.

## Two cohomology dimensions, one global section {#sec:cohomology}

::: {#prop:cohomology .proposition}
Give every port the stalk $\mathbb{R}$ and every seam the identity restriction,
and let $\delta^{0}$ be the resulting coboundary on the seam graph. Then
$\dim H^{0} = {{SHEAF_H0}}$ (the constants), $\dim H^{1} = {{SHEAF_H1}}$, and
$\delta^{0}$ has rank {{SHEAF_COBOUNDARY_RANK}}, so the Euler characteristic of the
graph is {{SHEAF_EULER_CHARACTERISTIC}}. Adding the twenty faces as two-cells,
with $\delta^{1}$ the signed face-seam incidence, $\delta^{1}$ has rank
{{SHEAF_FACE_COBOUNDARY_RANK}}: the surface has Betti numbers
{{SHEAF_SURFACE_BETTI}} and Euler characteristic
{{SHEAF_SURFACE_EULER_CHARACTERISTIC}}, and OPH's oriented faces sum to a
fundamental cycle.
:::

The probe reports both coboundaries' shapes and ranks in exact arithmetic, all
the cohomology dimensions, both Euler characteristics, the check that
$\delta^{1}\delta^{0} = 0$, and the dimensions of the same quantities for a chain
sheaf used as a control.

The two numbers have concrete readings, which is the reason for reporting them
separately rather than as one identity. The global sections are the assignments
of equal values to adjacent ports, so $\dim H^{0} = {{SHEAF_H0}}$ says that the only way for
every seam to agree is for every port to carry the same value: the agreement is a
constant. The cohomology dimension $\dim H^{1} = {{SHEAF_H1}}$ measures seam
assignments modulo coboundaries of port assignments. Its value is the
cycle rank of the seam graph, $\lvert E\rvert - \lvert V\rvert + 1 = {{CARRIER_SEAMS}} - {{CARRIER_PORTS}} + 1$.
Every disagreement that a port assignment actually produces lies in the image of
$\delta^{0}$. Both dimensions are forced by the graph
[@hansen2019sheaves; @ghrist2014elementary].

The second half of [@prop:cohomology] reconciles two numbers the paper uses. The
graph's Euler characteristic is {{SHEAF_EULER_CHARACTERISTIC}}; the surface's,
which the angle-defect count of [@prop:defect] reaches through Gauss-Bonnet, is
{{SHEAF_SURFACE_EULER_CHARACTERISTIC}}. The difference is the faces: the
face-seam incidence has rank {{SHEAF_FACE_COBOUNDARY_RANK}}, equal to the cycle
rank, so the face boundaries span the graph's cycle space and no
first cohomology survives on the sphere. The oriented faces sum to a cycle --
every seam is traversed once in each direction. This checks the coherence of the
declared orientation used by the rotation and reflection count [@prop:symmetry].

## The symmetry group closes the census {#sec:symmetry}

::: {#prop:symmetry .proposition}
The automorphism group of the carrier has order {{SYMMETRY_GROUP_ORDER}}, is
closed under composition, and acts transitively on the {{CARRIER_PORTS}} ports,
the {{CARRIER_SEAMS}} seams and the {{CARRIER_FACES}} faces. It splits into
{{SYMMETRY_ROTATIONS}} rotations and {{SYMMETRY_REFLECTIONS}}
orientation-reversing symmetries, and
the Laplacian eigenspaces of dimensions {{SYMMETRY_EIGENSPACE_DIMENSIONS}} are irreducible under
it. Across all group elements and all {{SYMMETRY_CONFIGURATIONS}} total-two
configurations, the multiset of descent outcomes takes
{{SYMMETRY_OUTCOME_MULTISETS}} values, on {{SYMMETRY_ORBITS}} orbits of
configurations.
:::

The probe reports each part separately: the group order from an exhaustive
backtracking search, closure under composition, the orbit decomposition of ports,
seams and faces, the orientation split, the orbit ranks and
commutant dimensions of the three nontrivial eigenspaces against their
dimensions, and the equivariance of
the descent law over the full product of ${{SYMMETRY_GROUP_ORDER}}$ group
elements and ${{SYMMETRY_CONFIGURATIONS}}$ configurations.

The permutation representation is multiplicity-free. An invariant symmetric
operator acts as a scalar on each irreducible subspace. The subspace dimensions
{{SYMMETRY_EIGENSPACE_DIMENSIONS}} are fixed, and the eigenvalues can vary or
coincide across subspaces. The repair rule also commutes with relabelling. For
each group element, configuration, oriented seam and coin, repairing the
relabelled state and seam gives the relabelled result of the original repair.
The descent outcome multiset in [@sec:pr01] is therefore constant within each
of the {{SYMMETRY_ORBITS}} configuration orbits. The registry records these
checks in
`icosahedral-symmetry`, `eigenspace-irreducibility` and `law-equivariance`.

To test orientation, the probe compares the cyclic order of each face up to
cyclic permutation. The union of directed edges would lose the distinction
because each seam occurs in both directions on the closed surface. The
orientation-reversing class includes central inversion.

## Gaussian divergence from squared lattice distance {#sec:kl-metric}

::: {#prop:kl .proposition}
For two Gaussians of equal covariance $\Sigma$ centred at the origin and at a
lattice site $z$, the Kullback–Leibler divergence is half the squared
Mahalanobis norm of the separation; with $\Sigma = I$ in the default embedding,

$$
D_{\mathrm{KL}}\bigl(\mathcal{N}(0, I) \,\|\, \mathcal{N}(z, I)\bigr)
\;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\lVert z\rVert^{2} ,
$$ {#eq:kl}

and the IVM's first neighbour shell sits at squared norm
${{KL_STEP_SQUARED_DISTANCE}}$, so one step costs
${{KL_ONE_STEP}}$ nats and the sites $k\,m$ along a neighbour move $m$ cost
{{KL_NORM_SPHERE_1}}, {{KL_NORM_SPHERE_2}}, {{KL_NORM_SPHERE_3}} and
{{KL_NORM_SPHERE_4}} nats for $k = 1,\dots,4$, that is $4k^{2}$.
:::

Using [@eq:kl], the probe reports the divergence for each neighbour move, the
squared distance of a move, and the costs of the sites $k\,m$ for the first four
multiples [@kullback1951information].

At unit covariance in the default embedding, each lattice displacement gives
an integer multiple of the neighbour divergence. Rescaling an isotropic
covariance rescales each divergence inversely and preserves their ratios.
Taking the variance per coordinate equal to the squared prime-vector length
gives half a nat for a neighbour displacement. The quadratic sequence in
[@prop:kl] compares endpoints along a ray. Additive path costs would give a
linear sequence instead.

## The carrier's subgraphs are the solid's own counts {#sec:census}

::: {#prop:subgraph-census .proposition}
Counting the seam graph's subgraphs by exhaustion returns
{{CARRIER_TRIANGLES}} triangles, {{CARRIER_PENTAGONS}} induced (chordless)
five-cycles, {{CARRIER_PERFECT_MATCHINGS}} perfect matchings and
{{CARRIER_HAMILTONIAN_CYCLES}} Hamiltonian cycles counted as undirected edge sets,
and the first of those is exactly the number of faces the port chart was derived
from.
:::

The probe enumerates rather than quotes: every three-vertex subset for the
triangles, every five-vertex subset for the pentagons, and a memoized recursion
for the matchings and the Hamiltonian cycles, all from the adjacency matrix
alone. The triangle count is where two derivations meet. The carrier's faces were
*derived* from OPH's oriented base faces in [@sec:port-chart], and the census then
*rediscovers* {{CARRIER_TRIANGLES}} triangles in the graph that derivation
produced. A count that agreed by construction would prove nothing; this one is
computed from the adjacency and checked against the face list afterwards.

The induced five-cycles are the links of the twelve ports, one around each, and
so they are the twelve pentagonal faces of the dual dodecahedron, which is the
first place in the paper where the dodecahedron appears without being named. The perfect matchings
and the Hamiltonian cycles have no counterpart in any of the three programmes,
and that is the reason to report them: they are properties of the object all
three are about, available to anyone who can read its adjacency.

## Curvature is exact in units of pi {#sec:defect}

::: {#prop:defect .proposition}
Five equilateral triangles meet at every port, so each port carries an angle
defect of {{CARRIER_DEFECT_PER_PORT}} pi and the twelve ports carry
{{CARRIER_TOTAL_DEFECT}} pi in total, which is $2\pi\chi$ for a triangulated
sphere: Descartes' theorem on the total angular defect, the combinatorial form of
Gauss-Bonnet, read off the face list [@federico1982descartes].
:::

The probe computes each defect as twice pi minus the incident faces times pi over
three and returns it as an exact rational multiple of pi rather than as a decimal:
{{CARRIER_DEFECT_PER_PORT}} at each port, {{CARRIER_TOTAL_DEFECT}} in total,
against the surface Euler characteristic {{SHEAF_SURFACE_EULER_CHARACTERISTIC}}
that the cohomology of [@prop:cohomology] reaches from the other direction. OPH's derived chart is the icosahedron's triangulation, so the
angular-defect accounting Synergetics supplies for that solid applies unchanged:
the vertex figure the accounting measures is the very five-face figure every port
carries. The angle sum agrees with the Euler characteristic computed from
the given spherical triangulation. Registered as `angle-defect`.

## Euclidean chords come from the lowest Gaussian modes {#sec:spectral-chords}

::: {#prop:carrier-spectral-chords .proposition}
Use the edge-length-two regular icosahedral realisation of the carrier and
the centred intrinsic Gaussian with unit seam precision. For vertices $i,j$,
write $d_{ij}=e_i-e_j$, and let $P_-$ project onto the lowest positive
Laplacian eigenspace of eigenvalue $\lambda_-$. The squared Euclidean chord is
$$
\|x_i-x_j\|^2 = {{CHORD_VARIANCE_FACTOR}}\,
      d_{ij}^{T} P_- d_{ij}/\lambda_- .
$$
The full Gaussian contrast variance $d_{ij}^{T}L^+d_{ij}$ has a
squared-distance embedding of rank {{CHORD_FULL_RANK}}. The icosahedral
chords have rank {{CHORD_EMBEDDING_RANK}}, so no uniform scaling identifies
the full contrast variance with squared chord length.
:::

Centred regular polyhedra realise their coordinates in the first positive
Laplacian eigenspace [@osting2023extremal]. Here the exact coordinate matrix $X$
satisfies $LX=(5-\sqrt{5})X$ and $X^{T}X=2(5+\sqrt{5})I$. Substituting
$P_-=XX^{T}/[2(5+\sqrt{5})]$ gives the scale in
[@prop:carrier-spectral-chords]. The full covariance gives the effective
resistance [@klein1993resistance]. Choosing a reference port makes its
contrast covariance the inverse of a grounded Laplacian, which has full rank
on the remaining ports. The probe compares exact chords and resistances
across every pair and finds different ratios as graph distance increases.
The same lowest modes give the slowest nonconstant decay of the OPH mean-load

## What the agreements do not say

One identification relates OPH's *dynamics* to a free-energy *functional*: on
the binary shell each native repair minimises [@eq:fibre-vfe]
([@prop:conditioning]). It holds step by step on that shell. It leaves the load
energy of [@eq:energy] what it was, and it says nothing off the shell. Several of
the other identifications are statements about counting, and most of the rest
are statements about the carrier, its spectrum, its repair rule as an integer map,
the Gaussian free energy on it, and the sheaf whose Laplacian it is. Every one of
those is a statement about a finite object that both programmes can be given.

The remaining identifications are structural rather than numerical, and the paper
reports them with the same care: a symmetry group of order
{{SYMMETRY_GROUP_ORDER}} is exact arithmetic, a spanning-tree number is an exact
integer, and a cohomology dimension is an exact rank. What none of them supplies
is a claim that either programme's *learning* dynamics is the other's.

The next section gives explicit counterexamples to proposed identifications.
Several keep the same stationary law or equilibrium while changing the
compatible dynamics.
